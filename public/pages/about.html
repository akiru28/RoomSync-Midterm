About RoomSync

A Smarter Way to Manage Room & Lab Reservations

RoomSync is a centralized reservation system built for Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges. It replaces manual coordination and spreadsheet-based scheduling with a streamlined digital platform that gives students, faculty, and administrators real-time visibility into room and laboratory availability.

The system helps reduce scheduling conflicts and double-bookings. Every reservation request follows a clear approval workflow, supporting transparency and accountability across departments.
